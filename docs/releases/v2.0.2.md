# Hermes Finance Stack `v2.0.2`

Bugfix release resolving ambiguity between **total purchase amount** and **per-installment amount** when registering credit card installment purchases.

---

## What Was Fixed

### Installment Amount vs. Total Amount (`register_expense` / `POST /api/expenses`)
Previously, `register_expense` only accepted `totalAmount`, causing phrases like *"pagué con visa 12 cuotas de 2000"* to be stored as `totalAmount: 2000` and divided into 12 installments of `$166.67`.

- **Backend DTO & Service** (`CreateExpenseDto`, `ExpensesService.resolveExpenseTotalAmount`):
  - Added optional `installmentAmount` (value of **each** installment) and `amountType?: 'TOTAL' | 'PER_INSTALLMENT'` (default `'TOTAL'`).
  - When `installmentAmount` is provided or `amountType === 'PER_INSTALLMENT'`, `totalAmount` is deterministically computed as `amount × totalInstallments` (e.g., `2000 × 12 = 24000`), and all `SHARED` allocations are calculated on that resolved total.
- **MCP Tool (`register_expense`)**:
  - Exposes `installmentAmount` and `amountType` with clear descriptions so Hermes never attempts manual LLM multiplication.
- **Hermes `SOUL.md`**:
  - Distinguishes *"12 cuotas de 2000"* (`installmentAmount` / `PER_INSTALLMENT`) from *"24000 en 12 cuotas"* (`TOTAL`), asks one short clarifying question when ambiguous, and confirms both values (`$24.000 ARS (12 cuotas de $2.000)`).
- **Unit Tests**:
  - 4 new Vitest specs in `expenses.service.spec.ts` (**42/42 tests passing** across 9 suites).
